Goal

Run paid Meta campaigns for many clients inside one ad account: cheap engagement for awareness and social proof, message campaigns for leads.

How I worked

Gave every client its own budgeted campaign and compared all of them on one metric, cost per result, so weak campaigns were easy to spot. Tracked KPIs in Power BI dashboards and trained new team members on the process.

What the data shows

Across 16 message campaigns the average cost was about 5 EGP per conversation, and early tests ran as high as 7.7 EGP. Refining targeting and budget brought the best campaign down to 1.98 EGP.

Flagship result

One client message campaign delivered 1,071 conversations at 1.98 EGP each, about 60% below the account average, reaching 102K people on roughly 2.1K EGP.

Engagement at scale

89 engagement campaigns averaged about 0.49 EGP per engagement. The best delivered 11.7K engagements for about 1.1K EGP (0.10 EGP each).

International Building Center

Freelance media buyer · Jun 2026 – now · Professional courses & certifications
1,782message leads
~7 EGPcost per lead
12.6K EGPad spend on leads
200K+ EGPbookings revenue

Goal

Turn Meta ads into real course enquiries on Messenger/WhatsApp, cheaply enough to scale, and into paid bookings.

How I worked

Ran message-conversation campaigns in short test rounds, reviewed each ad set's cost per lead every few days, and moved budget to whatever was cheapest.

What I tested

Interest audiences, lookalike lists, offer-led vs. general creative, then videos vs. photos. Ad sets that barely delivered were stopped within days; a one-message ad set was cut after 68 EGP.

Result

Early interest audiences cost about 5 EGP per lead vs. 12 EGP for the offer ad set, so budget followed the winners. Monthly spend grew from ~2.4K to ~5K EGP as lead cost held, and the leads converted into 200K+ EGP of bookings.

Personal project · Power BI, Power Query, DAX
15Kemployees analysed
3,571left the company (~24%)
61.3%average satisfaction

Goal

Find out why people leave and what keeps them, across turnover, satisfaction, promotions and working hours.

How I worked

Cleaned the dataset in Power Query, built the measures in DAX, then added slicers for department, promotion status, employment status and monthly hours so anyone can explore it.

What I found

Sales had the highest turnover (1,014 leavers). Low-salary staff left at about 30% vs. under 10% for high salaries, and satisfaction falls as pay falls (about 64% to 60%). Only 2.1% were ever promoted.
